## Talking Points for Interview

### "Why ActiveSG specifically?"
The transition from native app to web platform is one of the more interesting civic product problems in Singapore right now. They solved the fairness problem correctly — balloting is demonstrably better. But the migration created UX debt: payment friction, notification gaps, the physical-digital seam at the gantry. That's a specific, solvable set of problems. And the team is small enough that a designer has real ownership.

### "Walk me through your design process"
Lead with the Claims & Expense redesign (most end-to-end), but anchor it in the research starting point — stakeholder interviews with 20+ employees before a single wireframe. Then connect to what OGP values: "I don't start designing until I understand the constraints. For ActiveSG, that means the sports centre, not the Figma file."

### "How do you work with engineers?"
The Constitutional AI copy review system — designed the logic, worked with engineers on the implementation, understood the API constraints well enough to spec the edge cases. Also: building this prototype in the same stack the team ships in is a signal of where I sit on the design-engineering spectrum.

### "Tell me about a time you navigated a difficult stakeholder situation"
HR Analytics Dashboard — I was designing for HR but the real value crystallised when the sales team started using it for prospecting. Had to manage expectations on both sides while steering the product toward commercial viability without compromising the core HR use case. Result: became the primary sales prospecting tool and contributed 20%+ of platform engagement.

### "What would you do in your first 30 days?"
Week 1: Visit 3 sports centres. Observe real check-in flows. Talk to duty staff. Watch users interact with QR passes and gantries.
Week 2: Run 5 user interviews across key personas — regular court booker, parent booking for child, elderly gym user.
Week 3: Map the current state vs. the intended state. Identify the biggest delta.
Week 4: First design sprint on the highest-priority gap with the team.
